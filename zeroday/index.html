HOW TO PLAY

Every day there is one hidden cybersecurity word. You get 6 probes to crack it. Each probe fills the trace; at 100% you’re caught.

Type any letters to fill the row and press ENTER. Each letter comes back as:

P
Locked in. Right letter, right place.
H
Leaked. In the word, wrong place.
X
Denied. Not in the word.

You always see the word’s category and length. Stuck? Decrypt the hint, but it costs you one probe.
