Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how to make it go away.

EFFECTIVE DATE
October 17, 2026
Blackbat is operated by Milind Bhatt and Owen Chan. For any question about this policy, your data, or to exercise the rights described below, contact blackbat.terminal@gmail.com. For the purposes of the GDPR, we are the data controller for the personal data described here.

// WHAT_WE_COLLECT

Account information

When you sign up, we (via our authentication provider, Clerk) collect your email address, and if you sign in with Apple or Google, the name and email those providers share with us. If you sign up with a password, Clerk stores it in hashed form — we never see or store your raw password.

Profile & learning data

Your username, chosen experience level and interests, XP, streaks, hearts, quiz and lesson history, mastery/spaced-repetition progress, badges, and placement assessment results, and the avatar you picked. All of this is stored by Convex, our database provider, and linked to your account.

Friends & social features

If you add friends in-app, we store the connection (who requested, who accepted) and enough of each friend's profile (username, XP, streak, recent activity) to display it to their friends.

Purchases & subscriptions

If you subscribe to Blackbat Pro or buy a certification pack, our payment processor (RevenueCat, on top of the App Store) shares your subscription status, product IDs, and transaction IDs with us so we can unlock premium features. We do not receive or store your card number — that's handled entirely by Apple.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, we store a push notification token and your reminder preferences (which reminders, what time) so we can send them.

Usage analytics, screen recordings & crash reports

From version 1.0.10, the app sends usage data to PostHog, our analytics provider, so we can see where people get stuck and fix bugs: which screens you open and which actions you take (for example finishing a lesson or picking a plan on the subscription screen), your device type, operating system, app version and approximate location from your IP address, error reports when something crashes, and recordings of how you move through the app's screens. Recordings hide everything you type, including your email and password. This data is linked to your Blackbat account ID and a few account details (whether you have Pro, your matched role, learning goal, experience level and daily goal), never to your name or email.

You can switch all of this off at any time in Settings > Privacy > Share usage analytics and crash reports. That stops analytics, recordings and crash reports from that device straight away.

// WHAT_WE_DON'T_COLLECT

We do not access your camera or photo library, precise location, contacts, or microphone. Our analytics (above) only covers how you use Blackbat: we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't use your data for advertising. Profile pictures in Blackbat are chosen from a set of built-in avatar presets, not uploaded from your device.

// HOW_WE_USE_YOUR_DATA

To run the app you're using: authenticate you, save your progress, calculate streaks/XP/leaderboards, unlock premium content you've paid for, show your friends what you're up to, and send reminders you've opted into. Unless you switch it off, we also use usage analytics, screen recordings and crash reports to find and fix bugs and to improve lessons and the sign-up flow. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles of you.

// THIRD-PARTY_SERVICES

Who we share data with, and why

  • Clerk — authentication (email, name, login method)
  • Convex — our database and file storage (everything listed above)
  • RevenueCat & Apple's App Store / Google Play — subscription and purchase processing
  • PostHog — usage analytics, screen recordings and crash reports (see above; can be switched off in Settings)
  • Google AdMob — served ads in app versions before 1.0.9 only (see below)

Each of these processes data only to provide their specific service to us — none of them are permitted to use your Blackbat data for their own purposes.

// ADVERTISING

Blackbat no longer shows ads: from version 1.0.9 every account is on Blackbat Pro and the ad SDK has been removed. The rest of this section applies only if you still use an older version of the app.

In versions before 1.0.9, free accounts see two kinds of ads, both served by Google AdMob: a banner below some practice questions, and optional rewarded ads you can watch to skip a short cooldown. We request non-personalized ads only — we don't ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, and we don't share your Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) or any cross-app tracking data with Google or anyone else. Blackbat Pro removes ads entirely.

Non-personalized ads still involve storing and reading an identifier on your device. If you're in the EEA, the UK, or a US state with a comparable privacy law, we show you Google's consent form (via the Google User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested, and we don't request ads unless that form has been completed. You can reopen it and change your answer at any time from Settings > Ad privacy choices.

// DATA_RETENTION_&_DELETION

You can permanently delete your account at any time from Profile > Settings > Delete account — no email required. This immediately and permanently removes your profile, progress, streaks, avatar, and friend connections, and deletes your usage analytics, screen recordings and crash reports from PostHog. We retain purchase and transaction records after deletion, as required for financial recordkeeping and fraud prevention, but these are no longer linked to an active, usable account.

While your account is open we keep your profile and learning data so the app can work — there's no fixed expiry, because streaks and mastery history are the product. Purchase and transaction records are kept for 7 years to meet tax and financial recordkeeping obligations. Push notification tokens are discarded once they stop being valid or you turn reminders off. Screen recordings are deleted after 30 days, and usage analytics and crash reports after 1 year. Backups containing deleted data roll off within 30 days.

// LEGAL_BASIS_&_WHERE_DATA_GOES

Why we're allowed to process it (GDPR Art. 6)

  • Contract — your account, progress, purchases, friends and leaderboard entries: we can't provide the app you asked for without them.
  • Consent — push notifications, appearing on public leaderboards, and ads consent where required. You can withdraw any of these in-app at any time, without losing access to the rest of Blackbat.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the service secure, preventing abuse and fraud, keeping the financial records above, and usage analytics, screen recordings and crash reports to fix bugs and improve the app. You can object to the analytics at any time by switching it off in Settings > Privacy.

International transfers

Blackbat is operated from Australia, and our providers (Clerk, Convex, RevenueCat, PostHog, Google) process data on servers in the United States and other countries. Where data leaves the EEA or UK, those transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum), which each of these providers has in place with us.

// YOUR_RIGHTS

Depending on where you live, you have rights to access a copy of your data, correct it, export it in a portable form, delete it, restrict or object to certain uses of it, and withdraw any consent you've given. You can exercise most of these yourself in-app (edit your profile, change notification and leaderboard settings, delete your account); for anything else, email blackbat.terminal@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of under the CCPA/CPRA on either front. We won't discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights. If you're in the EEA or UK and you think we've got something wrong, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

// CHILDREN'S_PRIVACY

Blackbat is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

// SECURITY

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) between the app and our backend. Access to production data is limited to what's needed to operate the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we don't store payment card data, and we minimize what we collect in the first place.

// CHANGES_TO_THIS_POLICY

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of Blackbat after a change means you accept the updated policy.
